Before you go

Documents

No visa needed for Indians. Carry both passports anyway: the casino requires them.

Money

UPI works at many shops through Fonepay QR codes. Also carry a forex card, and check the current limits on bringing ₹200 and ₹500 notes into Nepal.

Weather

Sunny days around 18 to 20°C. Nights are close to freezing, colder in Nagarkot. Pack down jackets and warm layers.
