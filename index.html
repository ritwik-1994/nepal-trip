Before you go

Documents

No visa for Indians. Carry both passports: the casino requires them.

Money

UPI works at many shops through Fonepay QR codes. Carry a forex card and some cash in Nepali rupees for gate tickets. Check the current limits on bringing ₹200 and ₹500 notes into Nepal.

Weather

Sunny days around 18 to 20°C. Nights near freezing, colder in Nagarkot. Pack down jackets.

Still estimates

Transport, food, massage, gate fees and the casino's New Year's Eve rules. Travel times are approximate.
